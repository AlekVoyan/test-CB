Kestrel Dosing System — User Manual v1

1. Introduction
This manual covers two models of the Kestrel Dosing System: Model A and Model B.
Both models use the same 12 V power adapter, but they have different setup procedures and different load limits.
Read the section for your model before first use.

2. Setup: Model A
Step 1. Connect the power adapter.
Step 2. Press and hold the SETUP button for 3 seconds until the status light blinks green.
Step 3. Select channel 1 on the CHANNEL dial.
Do not select channel 2 during setup. Channel 2 is reserved for service mode.

3. Setup: Model B
Step 1. Connect the power adapter.
Step 2. Open the front cover.
Step 3. Set the MODE switch to position B.
Step 4. Press START.
Model B has no SETUP button. Holding START for more than 10 seconds resets Model B to factory settings.

4. Load Limits
Model A: the maximum load is 20 units.
Model B: the maximum load is 12 units under normal conditions.
If the load exceeds the maximum, the unit stops automatically and the status light blinks red.
The minimum load for both models is 2 units.
For continuous operation, keep the load at or below 75% of the maximum.

5. Capacity and Environment
Model A reservoir capacity: 5 liters.
Model B reservoir capacity: 8 liters.
Operating temperature for both models: 5°C to 35°C.

6. Exceptions
Exception: Model B may operate up to 15 units for no more than 5 minutes when ambient temperature is below 20°C.
There is no exception for Model A. Its maximum load must never be exceeded.

7. Maintenance
Clean the dosing nozzle of Model A every 15 days.
Clean the dosing nozzle of Model B every 30 days.
Replace the filter cartridge of either model every 6 months.

8. Troubleshooting
Status light blinks red: reduce the load, then press SETUP (Model A) or START (Model B).
Status light is off: check the power adapter connection.
