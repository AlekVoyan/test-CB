Nimbus Air Purifier — Owner's Guide

1. Models
This guide covers Nimbus Compact and Nimbus Pro.
Nimbus Compact is designed for rooms up to 25 square meters.
Nimbus Pro is designed for rooms up to 60 square meters.

2. Getting Started
Nimbus Compact: remove the plastic wrap from the filter, insert the filter, then press POWER.
Nimbus Pro: insert the filter, press POWER, then hold AUTO for 2 seconds to enable automatic mode.

3. Filter and Noise
Replace the HEPA filter of Nimbus Compact every 6 months.
Replace the HEPA filter of Nimbus Pro every 12 months.
Noise at the highest fan speed: Nimbus Compact 52 dB, Nimbus Pro 48 dB.
Night mode is available only on Nimbus Pro.
